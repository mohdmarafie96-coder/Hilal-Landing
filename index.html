FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I know my measurements will be accurate?

Follow the measuring guide above, and our team double-checks every measurement over WhatsApp before we start cutting.

How long does a pre-order take?

Usually two to three weeks after your measurements are confirmed.

Can I order for a full hospital or clinic team?

Yes — group and clinic orders are available. Reach out to us on WhatsApp.

What if the fit isn't right?

Since every set is cut to your measurements, we offer a free adjustment or remake within 14 days of delivery if the fit is off — no charge to you. Message us on WhatsApp with your order details and we will sort it out.

What fabric do you use?

A breathable, easy-care fabric built for long shifts and frequent washing.
